### Home-baked nut-free, dairy-free sponge cake (25 portions)

Base: a vegan sponge recipe from utopia.de (fetched 13 October 2026). Its listed ingredients for 12 portions are 180 g vegan margarine, 120 g sugar, 2 tsp vanilla sugar, 1 pinch of salt, 300 g flour, 250 ml plant milk and 2 tsp baking powder, baked at 180 °C top and bottom heat for about 50 to 60 minutes in a loaf tin. The page lists no nuts, nut oil, chocolate or egg in the base recipe. The doubling, the tray size, the bake time and the icing are this plan's own adaptation and are **unverified (estimate)**. A test bake of a small batch at least one week before the party is required.

Ingredients (one 30 x 40 cm tray, cut into 25 squares of about 6 x 8 cm):

- 360 g vegan margarine (dairy-free; label checked for milk, butter and nut traces)
- 240 g sugar
- 4 teaspoons vanilla sugar (label checked for milk and nut traces)
- 2 pinches of salt
- 600 g wheat flour (new sealed pack, label checked for nut traces)
- 500 ml unsweetened plain oat or soy drink (no milk, no nuts, no "may contain nuts"; no almond, hazelnut or other nut drink)
- 4 teaspoons baking powder (about 16 g, label checked for milk and nut traces)
- Topping (optional, own addition): 250 g icing sugar (new pack, label checked) mixed with 3 tablespoons of lemon juice until spreadable; about 400 g fruit in season (clementine or mandarin segments, or apple slices), added just before serving
- Dinosaur toppers: plastic figures, not eaten, placed on a board beside the cake

Method:

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top and bottom heat (or fan at the lower setting). Line a 30 x 40 cm tray with new baking paper.
2. Beat the margarine until soft and creamy. Add the sugar, vanilla sugar and salt, and beat until creamy.
3. Stir half of the flour into the mixture with the plant drink until combined. Fold in the rest of the flour mixed with the baking powder until just combined. Spread evenly in the tray.
4. Bake for about 35 to 45 minutes (unverified (estimate); the source gives 50 to 60 minutes for a loaf tin). A wooden skewer should come out clean. Cool for 15 minutes in the tray, then cool completely on a rack.
5. Mix the icing sugar with the lemon juice and spread over the cooled cake. Arrange the fruit on top just before serving.
6. Cut into 25 squares at 15:35. Keep the cake covered in a clean container, labelled "nut-free, dairy-free", away from any nut product. Bake the day before if possible.

Home kitchen controls: bake on a day when no nut products are opened, or clean the work surface, bowls, whisk, tin and utensils first. Use new packs of flour, sugar and icing sugar. Do not use chocolate, praline, marzipan, nut-containing mixes, nut drinks or nut oils. Serve on a clean board with a clean knife.

Allergen notes: vegan margarine, wheat flour, vanilla sugar, baking powder, the plant drink and icing sugar each carry a label check for nut and milk traces. Coconut is not used; ask the parents whether coconut should be avoided too. Gluten-free handling is not applicable, because no gluten-free constraint was stated.
