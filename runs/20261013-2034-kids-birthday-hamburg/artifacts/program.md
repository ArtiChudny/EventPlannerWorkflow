# Event Program

## Timing Overview

- **Start:** 14:00
- **End:** 17:00
- **Total duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes) on Saturday, 2027-03-20, indoors, Hamburg. Party run-of-show: 14:00 to 17:00. The party has no paid entertainer; the host runs all games.
- **Venue booking block (4-hour minimum, to be confirmed in writing):** The venue candidate Papijo Eventhaus (Hamburg-Eimsbüttel) sells its rooms as a minimum block of 4 hours ("feiert mindestens 4 Stunden ganz für euch", venue page fetched 2026-10-13). The 3-hour party therefore sits inside a 4-hour block. The block must be confirmed in writing before any booking, for example 13:30 to 17:30 (setup and cleanup in the extra time before and after the party) or 14:00 to 18:00.
- **Written confirmation of the slot (required before booking):** The venue must confirm in writing that 14:00 to 17:00 on Saturday 2027-03-20 is available inside the 4-hour block. The venue page does not state Saturday availability or time slots ("Termin und Startzeit wählen, auf Wunsch Stunden dazubuchen"). If the venue cannot confirm the slot, this run-of-show must be re-checked before any booking.
- **Cost of the 4th hour:** The plan assumes the extra hour is paid for within the 1,300 EUR budget, unless the venue confirms a 3-hour booking in writing. The venue plan carries the price.
- **Setup and cleanup:** Not part of the 14:00 to 17:00 run-of-show. They fall inside the booked block, outside the party window.
