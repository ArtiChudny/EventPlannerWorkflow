# Logistics and Staffing

Research date: 2026-10-13. Event: 7th birthday party (dinosaur theme), Saturday 2027-03-20, 14:00 to 17:00, 25 guests (15 children, 10 parents), Hamburg. Venue used for this plan: Papijo Eventhaus, Hamburg-Eimsbüttel, conditional. The venue is not booked. Capacity for 25, the 14:00 to 17:00 slot inside the 4-hour minimum block, and the own-cake rule must be confirmed in writing before booking (venue.md, requirements.md). No paid entertainer is planned.

Assumed times for logistics (not confirmed by the venue): venue access from 13:30 and exit by 17:30, i.e. the proposed 13:30 to 17:30 block (venue.md, decor-av.md). If the venue only offers 14:00 to 18:00, see Risks.

Run-of-show used here: program.md (14:00 to 17:00). catering.md now uses the same service times as program.md (food 15:05 to 15:35, cake 15:35 to 15:50), so the earlier timing conflict is resolved (Risks, R5).
