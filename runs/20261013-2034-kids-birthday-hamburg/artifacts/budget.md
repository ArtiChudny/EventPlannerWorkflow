# Budget

## Limit and Assumptions

- **Limit:** 1,300.00 EUR, total including the 10% contingency (requirements.md, Budget section). The limit applies to the grand total.
- **Currency:** EUR. All source figures are already in EUR, so no conversion was needed.
- **Contingency:** 10% of the subtotal (requirements.md does not state another rate).
- **Costs taken from:** the Recommended Pick and Cost Summary of venue.md (Recommended Pick), catering.md (Cost Summary), program.md (Cost Summary), decor-av.md (Cost Summary) and logistics.md (Cost Summary). Each cost is counted once, in the artifact that owns it.
- **Venue:** Papijo Eventhaus, 4-hour minimum block at 62.50 EUR per hour (venue page, fetched 2026-10-13). The package behind the rate, the Saturday price and the 2027 price are unverified (estimate). The venue is not booked and the slot, capacity for 25 and the own-cake rule are not yet confirmed in writing.
- **Excluded from the total:** the Papijo Bluetooth music box (included in venue hire, 0.00 EUR); the Heycater and MYMY catering comparisons (not selected); the venue speaker alternative (decor-av.md, not counted).
- **Not budgeted, no price found:** table and chair rental if the venue does not supply them (decor-av.md, R14); any venue cleaning fee or deposit (logistics.md, R8); taxi fallback; parking (public transport plan).
- **Artifacts present:** requirements.md, venue.md, catering.md, program.md, decor-av.md and logistics.md were all read and are current. No artifact is missing.
- **Conflicts between artifacts (no cost change made in this budget):**
  1. **Papijo capacity wording:** catering.md (Sources and Blockers) says the venue page's "Was ihr bekommt" section gives up to 40 seats in the whole house, while venue.md says the fetched page states up to 35 and that the "40" passage is not on the page. The owners must resolve this. The budget is not affected, but capacity for 25 is still unconfirmed.
  2. **Possible double count of napkins and cups:** decor-av.md tableware (plates, napkins, cups, 17.95 EUR) and catering.md disposables (napkins, serving boards or platters, cake board, 12.00 EUR) both list napkins. catering.md also says the host brings cups, while decor-av.md buys cups. The budget counts both rows as written. If one is removed, the total falls. Owners: decor-av-planner and catering-planner.
  3. **Speaker versus venue music box:** decor-av.md budgets a purchased speaker (59.00 EUR). It also says this speaker is dropped if the venue confirms its Bluetooth music box in writing. program.md refers to both options. The 59.00 EUR stays in the total until the venue confirms. This is a possible saving, not a conflict in amounts.
  4. **Time block:** logistics.md and decor-av.md assume a 13:30 to 17:30 block. program.md and venue.md agree that this block is unconfirmed. If the venue offers only 14:00 to 18:00, the 4-hour price is unchanged in this budget, but the timing changes.
