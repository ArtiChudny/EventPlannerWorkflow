- **Guests:** 25 in total (15 children and 10 parents). Written capacity confirmation for 25 from the venue is required before booking.
- **Mode:** Revision. The user chose to pursue Papijo Eventhaus. The paid entertainer is dropped. The venue must confirm in writing: (a) capacity for 25, (b) the 14:00 to 17:00 slot (Papijo sells a 4-hour minimum block), and (c) its own-cake rule (home-baked cake allowed).

## Recommended Pick

**Recommended: Papijo Eventhaus (Hamburg-Eimsbüttel).** Its own page states that guests may bring their own food and their own cake and decorations, and it is an indoor venue with a children's section. It is the venue the user has chosen to pursue.

**Must be confirmed in writing before booking (all open, not verified):**

1. **Capacity for 25 guests (15 children, 10 parents).** The page publishes up to 35 persons and up to 40 seats in the whole house. The venue must confirm 25 in writing.
2. **The 14:00 to 17:00 slot on Saturday 2027-03-20.** The page does not state Saturday availability or time slots. It sells a minimum of 4 hours. Ask whether a 13:30 to 17:30 block (setup and cleanup) can be booked, and confirm the price for that block in writing.
3. **Own-cake rule.** The page states own cake is allowed (FAQ). Confirm in writing that a home-baked, nut-free cake with a lactose-free option is allowed on 2027-03-20.
4. Also ask in writing: whether the price covers the 4-hour block, the deposit and cancellation terms, and accessibility details (not stated on the page).

**Estimated venue cost:** about 250 EUR (4 hours at the starting rate of 62.50 EUR per hour, from the page). The package behind the rate, the 2027 price and any deposit are unverified (estimate) and may be higher.

**Backup:** None verified. Loft 25 and Glückspilze do not state their food and cake rules on their pages, so neither can be recommended as a backup until those rules are confirmed in writing.
