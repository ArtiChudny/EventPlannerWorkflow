# Decor and AV Plan

## Decor Concept

- **Theme:** Dinosaurs, kid-friendly, for 15 children and 10 parents, Saturday 20 March 2027, 14:00 to 17:00, indoors in Hamburg. The room is Papijo Eventhaus, Hamburg-Eimsbüttel, which is conditional on written confirmation of capacity for 25, the 14:00 to 17:00 slot and the home-baked cake rule (per venue.md). Decor is planned for that room only.
- **Look:** Simple and bright. Dino-motif tableware (plates, napkins, cups) on the party tables, a small cluster of green, yellow and brown dinosaur balloons at the entrance and at the food table, and a dinosaur-style "Alles Gute zum Geburtstag" sign made from craft paper, hung from a string or clipped to a stand.
- **Venue rule for decorations:** The Papijo page states "Ihr könnt Deko, Kuchen, Snacks und Getränke selbst mitbringen" (you may bring your own decoration, cake, snacks and drinks), so bringing own decoration is permitted. The page does not state rules on balloons, tape or wall fixings. Until the venue confirms in writing, the plan uses only removable items: no candles, no confetti, no glitter, no open flames, and no tape, stickers or pins on walls, doors or windows.
- **Balloons:** Air-filled balloons only, with no helium. Balloons are tied to chair backs or table legs.
- **Setup and cleanup:** The Papijo page has a 4-hour minimum booking. The plan asks for a booking covering setup and cleanup (for example 13:30 to 17:30), so decoration setup falls inside the booked time. Availability of that slot is an open item in venue.md.
- **Shoe-free house:** Papijo is a shoe-free house (per the venue page). Decorations are placed at table height or on the floor only where guests can reach them safely in socks or slippers.

## Sound, Light and AV

- **Speaker (planned purchase, budgeted):** One Bluetooth karaoke-style party speaker, 30 W, with two wireless microphones, bought for the party. It runs on its own battery and connects over Bluetooth, so it needs no venue power socket. Cost: 59.00 EUR (unverified (estimate), no vendor named, see Cost Summary). This speaker is in the Cost Summary.
- **Speaker (venue music box, unconfirmed option only):** The Papijo page lists "Discolicht, Schwarzlicht, Bluetooth-Musikbox" as equipment (https://www.papijo.de/partyraum-eimsbuettel-mieten/). This is not relied on. It is used only if the venue confirms in writing that the Bluetooth music box is available on 20 March 2027 and that a phone can be connected to it. If confirmed, the purchased speaker can be dropped, and the Decor and AV total falls by 59.00 EUR. Cost: 0 EUR, not counted in the total until confirmed.
- **Microphones:** The two wireless microphones of the purchased speaker serve the host, who runs the dinosaur games, and one helper. No separate sound system is planned.
- **Music rules:** The Papijo page states no rule on music or speakers. Noise limits and curfew are not stated (per venue.md). Keep music at conversation level during food and at a moderate level during games. Permission for music in the room is unverified (estimate) until confirmed in writing.
- **Power:** The purchased speaker runs on its own battery and needs no mains connection. Mains socket positions in the room are not stated by the venue and are not needed for the purchased speaker.
- **Light:** Use the room's existing lights. Papijo lists disco light and blacklight as equipment provided. These are not planned for the party and add no cost. Lights are not dimmed unless the venue confirms that dimming is possible.
- **Projector and screen:** Not applicable. No film or presentation is planned, so no projector or screen is needed.

## Photo and Video

- **Photo coverage:** One parent is asked in advance to take photos during the party with their own phone. This covers arrival, the cake moment, the games and the goodie-bag handover. No professional photographer is booked, because no verified photographer price was found for this plan.
- **Video coverage:** No video recording is planned. The phone photos cover the key moments.
- **Consent:** Parents are asked on the invitation whether photos of their children may be shared. No photos are published unless the parents agree.
- **Cost:** 0 EUR. Phones are the parents' own devices.
