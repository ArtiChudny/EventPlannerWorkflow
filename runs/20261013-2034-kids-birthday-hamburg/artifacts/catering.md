- **Other dietary needs:** None stated (Q&A answer 8). Parents' needs are not specified and none are assumed.
- **Budget:** 1,300 EUR total including 10% contingency (requirements.md). The catering share is not allocated here; budget-aggregator decides the split. The catering total in this plan is 150.00 EUR (unverified (estimate)).

## Menu Proposal

Service plan for 14:00 to 17:00 at Papijo Eventhaus (own food, drinks and cake allowed per the venue page). Only cold items are served. The times below match the party programme (program.md): finger food and drinks break 15:05 to 15:35, cake cut at 15:35 within the cake block 15:35 to 15:50.

Food handling: every purchased product is checked by the buyer for milk, butter, cream, whey, egg (if any), nuts and "may contain nuts" traces. Empty packs or labels are kept so the parents of the nut-allergy child can check them on the day. Cold items stay in the fridge or a cool box until serving. The venue is shoe-free (thick socks or slippers recommended), which is noted for guests.

- **Welcome drink (arrival, from 14:00):** Still water and apple juice in sealed bottles, poured into cups brought by the host. No milk-based drinks, no nut-based drinks.
- **Finger food and drinks break (15:05 to 15:35, cold, on platters, no plates needed; for 25 guests, reduced portions):**
  - Vegetable sticks (cucumber, carrot, sweet pepper, about 0.8 kg) with a dairy-free dip (label checked, no yoghurt, cream or cheese, for example a plain oil-based dip).
  - Fruit pieces in season in March (apple and pear slices, clementine segments, grapes cut lengthwise for the younger children, banana, about 1.0 kg). Fruit only, no nut toppings.
  - About 40 small sandwich halves (20 sandwiches cut in half) on dairy-free bread (label checked, no butter): ham or turkey; vegetarian option with cucumber or tomato, no cheese.
  - About 1.0 kg cold cooked chicken or turkey pieces, unbreaded, without a milk-based marinade, served with ketchup (label checked).
  - Plain rice cakes and plain unsalted popcorn (one pack of each, label checked), served as the snack items during the same break.
- **Cake (cake block 15:35 to 15:50, cut at 15:35):** One home-baked sheet cake, vegan and nut-free by recipe, so it is also dairy-free. Cut into 25 squares at 15:35, after the food break. The same cake covers the nut-allergy child and the two lactose-free children, so no second cake is needed.
- **Drinks (throughout):** Still water (10 L), apple juice (8 L), orange juice (3 L), and cola or lemonade (4 L), all in sealed bottles or cans, about 25 litres in total. All guests may drink all drinks. Each drink is label-checked for milk and nut traces. No nut milk or nut-based drinks.
- **Dessert:** The cake is the only dessert. No ice cream, which avoids dairy and nut-flavour risks.
- **Allergen handling:** Put the nut-free and dairy-free items on separate, labelled platters. Use separate serving utensils. Serve the nut-allergy child's squares first from a labelled tray with a clean knife, before the cake is shared.

### Home-baked nut-free, dairy-free sponge cake (recipe for 25 portions)

Base: the vegan Rührkuchen recipe on utopia.de (fetched 2026-10-13). Its listed ingredients for 12 portions are 180 g vegan margarine, 120 g sugar, 2 tsp vanilla sugar, 1 pinch of salt, 300 g flour, 250 ml plant milk and 2 tsp baking powder, with 180 °C top and bottom heat for about 50 to 60 minutes in a loaf tin. The page lists no nuts, nut oil, chocolate or egg in the base recipe. Its nut and chocolate items appear only in optional variants, which are not used here. This plan doubles the batter for 25 portions and bakes it in one 30 x 40 cm tray. The doubling, the tray size, the bake time and the icing are this plan's own adaptation and are **unverified (estimate)**. A test bake of a small batch at least one week before the party is required.

The earlier lactose-free base that contained 250 g ground nuts was rejected, because it is not nut-free.

Ingredients (one 30 x 40 cm tray, cut into 5 x 5 = 25 squares of about 6 x 8 cm; doubled from the 12-portion base):
- 360 g vegan margarine (dairy-free; label checked for milk, butter and nut traces)
- 240 g sugar
- 4 teaspoons vanilla sugar (about 2 sachets, label checked for milk and nut traces)
- 2 pinches of salt
- 600 g wheat flour (new sealed pack, label checked for nut traces)
- 500 ml unsweetened plain oat or soy drink (label checked: no milk, no nuts, no "may contain nuts"; no almond, hazelnut or other nut drink)
- 4 teaspoons baking powder (about 16 g, one sachet, label checked for milk and nut traces)

Topping and decoration:
- 250 g icing sugar (new pack, label checked for milk and nuts), mixed with 3 tablespoons of lemon juice (about 45 ml) until spreadable (optional, own addition)
- About 400 g fruit in season in March (clementine or mandarin segments, drained if canned, or apple slices), washed, added just before serving
- Dinosaur toppers: plastic figures, not eaten, placed on a board beside the cake. No edible decorations are bought.

Method:
1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C top and bottom heat (or fan at the lower setting). Line a 30 x 40 cm tray with new baking paper.
2. Beat the margarine until soft and creamy. Add the sugar, vanilla sugar and salt, and beat until creamy.
3. Stir half of the flour into the mixture with the plant drink until combined. Fold in the rest of the flour mixed with the baking powder until just combined. Spread evenly in the tray.
4. Bake for about 35 to 45 minutes (unverified (estimate); the source gives 50 to 60 minutes for a loaf tin). A wooden skewer should come out clean. Cool for 15 minutes in the tray, then cool completely on a rack.
5. Mix the icing sugar with the lemon juice and spread over the cooled cake. Arrange the fruit on top just before serving.
6. Cut into 25 squares at 15:35, when the cake block starts. Keep the cake covered in a clean container, labelled "nut-free, dairy-free", away from any nut product. Bake the cake the day before the party if possible, so that it is fully cool and set on the day.

### Allergen check for the cake and food

| Ingredient | Nut risk | Dairy risk | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vegan margarine | Low (some brands made on shared lines) | Must be dairy-free by label | Buy a vegan margarine labelled milk-free and with no "may contain nuts" |
| Wheat flour | Traces possible at the mill | None | Buy a new sealed pack; avoid if the label says "may contain nuts" |
| Sugar, vanilla sugar, baking powder | Low | Low (vanilla sugar may be blended with milk powder) | Check each label for milk and nut traces |
| Plant drink (oat or soy) | Low; some brands have nut traces | Milk-free by definition | Plain unsweetened oat or soy drink with a label showing no milk and no nut traces. Do not use almond, hazelnut, chocolate or flavoured drinks |
| Salt | None | None | Standard table salt |
| Fruit (clementine, mandarin, apple) | None | None | Wash; drain canned fruit well |
| Icing sugar | Low | Low | Check label |
| Lemon juice | None | None | Bottled or fresh lemon juice |
| Dinosaur toppers | Not eaten | Not eaten | Keep separate; plastic figures are not food |
| Coconut (not used) | Not a legal nut in Germany, but some families avoid it | Dairy-free | Not used. Ask the parents whether coconut should be avoided too |
| Egg, soy, oat, wheat (in the cake) | Not nut allergens | Not dairy | The recipe contains no egg. Soy or oat drink and wheat are used. No soy, oat, wheat or egg allergy is stated (Q&A answer 8); parents should confirm this |

Home kitchen controls (cross-contact):
- Bake on a day when no nut products are opened, or clean the work surface, bowls, whisk, tin and utensils with hot water and detergent first.
- Use new packs of flour, sugar and icing sugar, not open packs that may have been used for nut baking.
- Do not use chocolate, praline, marzipan, nut-containing cake mixes, nut drinks or nut oils. Dark chocolate is excluded unless its label shows no milk and no nut traces.
- Serve the cake on a clean board, with a clean knife.

Gluten-free handling: Not applicable. No gluten-free constraint is stated in the requirements. The cake and bread contain wheat.

## Recommended Pick

**Self-prepared finger food, snacks, shop-bought drinks, and one home-baked, vegan, nut-free sponge cake that is also dairy-free. Catering total: 150.00 EUR (unverified (estimate)), 6.00 EUR per head. This is the figure for the budget.**

Sources for the pick: recipe base https://utopia.de/ratgeber/veganer-ruehrkuchen-ein-schnelles-und-einfaches-rezept/ (fetched 2026-10-13; no nuts, no egg, doubled and bake time adapted, unverified); drinks price references https://www.supermarktcheck.de/direktsaft/produkte/ (fetched 2026-10-13; undated listing); venue own-food rule https://www.papijo.de/partyraum-eimsbuettel-mieten/ (fetched 2026-10-13).

Reasons:
- It is the only option found that covers both the nut-free cake and the lactose-free option with one cake, because the recipe has no nuts, no eggs and no milk products. No bakery has confirmed nut-free production in writing, so no bakery is used.
- The venue's rules allow it: food, drinks and cake may be brought (Papijo page, fetched 2026-10-13).
- It is the lowest cost: MYMY (about 538 EUR) and Heycater (about 776 EUR) both exceed the room left after the venue, and neither states nut-free or lactose-free handling.
- Drinks come from a shop, because the venue page does not say it sells drinks.

Blockers and open questions:
1. **Venue confirmation:** Papijo Eventhaus must confirm in writing that 25 guests (15 children, 10 parents) fit. The venue page is inconsistent: the "Was ihr bekommt" section says up to 40 seats in the whole house, and the FAQ says up to 35. The venue must also confirm the Saturday 14:00 to 17:00 slot (4-hour minimum) and that the home-baked cake and own food may be brought on 2027-03-20.
2. **Test bake:** The doubled vegan cake (25 portions, 30 x 40 cm tray, bake time) is an unverified adaptation. A test bake is needed at least one week before the party.
3. **Label checks:** Every purchased product must be checked by the buyer for milk, dairy and nut traces. Neither MYMY nor Heycater states allergen information, so neither is used.
4. **Parents' confirmation:** Ask the parents of the nut-allergy child to approve the recipe and the label checks, and to say whether coconut should also be avoided. Ask all parents whether any egg, soy, oat or wheat allergy exists.
5. **Prices:** All costs are estimates (unverified (estimate)) except the vendor prices marked sourced. The buyer should price the shopping list before the budget is finalised.
6. **Service times:** The times 14:00, 15:05, 15:35 and 15:50 must match the party programme slots (program.md).
