Overview

This is a corporate year-end party for 80 guests on Saturday 12 December 2026, from 18:00 to 23:00, in central Berlin. It is an elegant but relaxed winter evening with a buffet dinner, a live band, a short award ceremony (about 20 minutes, 5-8 awards) and a photo booth, all fully indoors. The recommended venue is Balcony am Fernsehturm (Alexanderstrasse 7, at Alexanderplatz), which has ample capacity, a stage with sound and lighting, a step-free entrance via four lifts, and allows outside catering. The planned budget is 10,445.00 EUR before contingency and 11,489.50 EUR with a 10% contingency, which is 510.50 EUR under the 12,000 EUR limit. The margin is thin because almost every figure is an estimate that real quotes could change.

Open items to settle before booking: the accessible toilets, the venue price, and what the venue includes (furniture, technician, projector).

Confirmed Requirements

  • Event: corporate year-end party with live music and a short award ceremony.
  • Date and time: Saturday 12 December 2026, 18:00-23:00 (5 hours).
  • Location: central Berlin with good public transport.
  • Guests: 80.
  • Style: elegant but relaxed winter party, no strict dress code. Fully indoor.
  • Budget: 12,000 EUR maximum, all-inclusive and including a 10% contingency, so planned costs must stay at or below 10,800 EUR before contingency.

  • Music: live band or jazz/acoustic duo. A DJ only if the budget allows.
  • Program: award ceremony of about 20 minutes with 5-8 awards, plus basic decoration, sound and lights.
  • Services: coat check and staff included in the budget.
  • Accessibility: step-free entrance and accessible toilets are required. Step-free access to the stage is not required; a ramp or portable stage ramp is a nice-to-have, to be confirmed with the venue.
  • Not included: guest travel or accommodation. Language of the program (English and/or German) is up to the company.

Menu proposal

Dish names are illustrative; the final menu is fixed with the quoted caterer.

  • Welcome: sparkling wine or alcohol-free elderflower sparkle, with vegan canapes (beetroot hummus cups, tomato-basil crostini on gluten-free bread).
  • Starters: seasonal winter salad with roasted root vegetables and pomegranate (vegan, gluten-free), pumpkin-ginger soup in cups (vegan, gluten-free), cheese and antipasti board (vegetarian, gluten-free cracker option).
  • Mains: vegan lentil and vegetable stew or lentil patties with potatoes (vegan, gluten-free), vegetarian gratin or baked polenta with mushrooms (vegetarian, gluten-free), plus one meat or fish main (for example chicken with roasted vegetables, allergens labelled). Sides: roasted potatoes, seasonal vegetables, rice or quinoa. Two parallel buffet lines serve 80 guests within 50 minutes.
  • Dessert (from 19:45, open until about 21:00): fruit and vegan chocolate mousse (gluten-free), baked apple, gluten-free brownie, cookies and stollen; coffee and tea.
  • Late snack (21:15-22:15): mini falafel, mini quiche or vegan sliders, with a vegan and a gluten-free choice, self-service.
  • Drinks (18:00-23:00), about 14 EUR per head: sparkling wine on arrival, one house red and one white wine, Pils, alcohol-free beer, still and sparkling water, juices, soft drinks, coffee and tea.
  • Service notes: every dish labelled for vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and main allergens. Gluten-free dishes are prepared separately and served first with their own utensils. Order well ahead for a December Saturday (5-7 business days is the minimum advised for gluten-free). Buffet tables and the bar should be step-free with wheelchair-passable aisles, and a staff member can serve guests who cannot queue.
