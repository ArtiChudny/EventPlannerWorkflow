# Corporate Year-End Party - Berlin, 12 December 2026

## Overview

This is a corporate year-end party for 80 guests on Saturday 12 December 2026, from 18:00 to 23:00, in central Berlin. It is an elegant but relaxed winter evening with a buffet dinner, a live band, a short award ceremony (about 20 minutes, 5-8 awards) and a photo booth, all fully indoors. The recommended venue is Balcony am Fernsehturm (Alexanderstrasse 7, at Alexanderplatz), which has ample capacity, a stage with sound and lighting, a step-free entrance via four lifts, and allows outside catering. The planned budget is 10,445.00 EUR before contingency and 11,489.50 EUR with a 10% contingency, which is 510.50 EUR under the 12,000 EUR limit. The margin is thin because almost every figure is an estimate that real quotes could change. Open items to settle before booking are the accessible toilets, the venue price, and what the venue includes (furniture, technician, projector).

## Confirmed Requirements

- Event: corporate year-end party with live music and a short award ceremony.
- Date and time: Saturday 12 December 2026, 18:00-23:00 (5 hours).
- Location: central Berlin with good public transport.
- Guests: 80.
- Style: elegant but relaxed winter party, no strict dress code. Fully indoor.
- Budget: 12,000 EUR maximum, all-inclusive and including a 10% contingency, so planned costs must stay at or below 10,800 EUR before contingency.
- Food and drink: seated or buffet dinner plus snacks, with wine, beer and soft drinks. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests are all assumed present (no counts were given).

- Services: coat check and staff included in the budget.
- Accessibility: step-free entrance and accessible toilets are required. Step-free access to the stage is not required; a ramp or portable stage ramp is a nice-to-have, to be confirmed with the venue.
- Not included: guest travel or accommodation. Language of the program (English and/or German) is up to the company.
