## Permits and Insurance

- **Alcohol licence / Gestattung:** In Berlin, a "Gestattung aus besonderem Anlass" is needed when alcohol is served commercially and temporarily at an event for the public or a specific group; fee 11 to 869 EUR, about 2 weeks processing, applied for at the district Ordnungsamt at least 2 weeks before. Source: https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/327491/. The page does not say whether it applies to a closed company party where a licensed caterer serves drinks. Assumption (unverified (estimate)): not needed when the caterer or venue holds the licence; the event lead must get written confirmation from the caterer. Not costed; if the company must apply, up to 869 EUR would be due and would come out of the contingency.
- **Music rights (GEMA):** Anyone who plays or performs music publicly must register it with GEMA beforehand; the source gives about 100 EUR GEMA fees for 80 guests with 5 hours of music in a 100 m2 room (sports club example). Source: https://www.leinetal24.de/leben/geld/profitipps-zur-weihnachtsfeier-musik-nutzen-ohne-gema-aerger-zr-93445649.html (page verified). Whether a company party counts as public and whether the venue already holds a GEMA licence is not verified; planned 100 EUR (unverified (estimate), as the example room and event differ). Ask the venue and the band who registers.
- **Noise / outdoor use:** Not applicable beyond the venue's own rules: the event is fully indoor and no outdoor use is planned. No official noise rule was found; the venue's noise limit and curfew are not stated (unverified (estimate)); confirm before booking.
- **Event liability insurance:** No official page was searched and no legal requirement is claimed. Normal practice is that the organiser holds cover and the venue may ask for proof (unverified (estimate)). Lean plan: check the company's existing business liability policy first (0 EUR). If it does not cover the event, a one-day policy would be needed (roughly 150 EUR, unverified (estimate), no quote) and is not in the planned total; it would be paid from the contingency. Vendors (caterer, band, technician) should show their own liability cover.
- **Data protection:** The photographer needs a notice at the entrance (decor and AV plan); no cost.
