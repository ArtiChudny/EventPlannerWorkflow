# Decor and AV Plan

## Decor Concept

Theme: elegant but relaxed winter party, fully indoor, no strict dress code, 80 guests, at Balcony am Fernsehturm (recommended venue, far larger than needed). Source for venue: https://venuu.com/de/de/venues/balcony-am-fernsehturm

- Palette: deep green, warm white and brass/gold accents with natural winter materials (fir branches, pine cones, dried orange slices, cream fabric). Calm and warm.
- Lighting mood: warm white only, using the venue's in-house lighting. LED candles only (no open flame), no confetti, no fog or smoke effects. Venue decor rules are not stated on the venue pages: unverified (estimate); confirm before booking.
- Zoning: define a smaller zone for 80 guests with the stage at one end, dining tables in the middle and a lounge corner (the venue's own lounge furniture, see Rentals). Use plants or fabric drapes as dividers. Whether a partial layout is offered is unverified (estimate).
- Tables: dinner tables with cream linen, a low centerpiece of fir branches and LED tea lights, and one printed menu card per place (menu content comes from the catering plan).
- Stage: the venue stage with a simple backdrop (plants or fabric drape) and a small sign with the company name and year. Step-free stage access is no longer a hard requirement. A portable stage ramp is a nice-to-have: ask the venue whether one can be provided and at what cost (stage height and ramp are not stated on the venue pages: unverified (estimate)).
- Awards: a small table beside the stage with the 5-8 trophies/certificates.
- Accessibility: keep at least 1.2 m free aisles, leave open wheelchair spaces at several tables, and cover cables with mats or ramps. The venue entrance is step-free with lifts per the venue pages; accessible toilets are still to be confirmed by the venue (see the venue plan).

## Sound, Light and AV

Program needs: live band or jazz/acoustic duo, microphones for a roughly 20-minute award ceremony with 5-8 awards, a projector or screen for award slides, background music during dinner, and basic lighting for music and awards.

Provided by the venue (listing; use it first): projector/beamer, 7 m LED screen, sound system, PA, lighting, stage with backstage, dance floor. Sources: https://venuu.com/de/de/venues/balcony-am-fernsehturm and https://www.eventlocations.com/de/venues/balcony-am-fehrnsehturm-berlin. The venue plan states that no page confirms whether the projector, screen or furniture are in the base price. This plan assumes the in-house AV is included and requires written confirmation in the venue quote. The technician is treated as an extra cost until confirmed.

Plan:
- Use the in-house PA, stage lighting, projector and LED screen for the band, the ceremony and the award slides. No separate sound, light or projector rental and no mood-lighting add-ons are planned.
- Microphones: the venue pages do not list microphones. Budget one rented wireless/handheld microphone set for the host and presenters at 150 EUR flat (unverified (estimate), the D&F Eventverleih Berlin listing page was fetched but did not show this price). This line is dropped if the venue confirms microphones in the quote.
- Technician (setup, soundcheck, operating through the ceremony): 400 EUR for the evening (unverified (estimate), typical Berlin event technician rate for 5-8 hours). Ask the venue first; ambiente light & Sound is an alternative (quote on request).
- Sound check and load-in need access before 18:00; the band must not bring a duplicate PA (align the band rider with the program plan).
- Power: no outdoor equipment, so no weather protection is needed (weather outlook: indoor event, risk low). Venue power capacity is not stated: unverified (estimate); confirm.
- Noise: curfew and noise limit are not stated; keep music to the planned 23:00 end and confirm with the venue.

## Photo and Video

- Coverage: one photographer for about 3 hours (arrival and welcome from 18:00, award ceremony and band highlights until about 21:00), covering awards, a small photo spot near the stage and candid shots of dinner and music.
- Deliverables: 80-150 edited images in an online gallery within about 1-2 weeks.
- Video: not planned (budget).
- Guests are informed about photography with a notice at the entrance (GDPR).
- Price basis: Berlin corporate event photo and video packages are given as 800-3000 EUR depending on duration and services (XPulse, https://www.xpulse.space/corporate-event-photography-in-berlin-blog). Planning figure 800 EUR, the low end of that range, for photo coverage only (exact quote unverified (estimate)).
