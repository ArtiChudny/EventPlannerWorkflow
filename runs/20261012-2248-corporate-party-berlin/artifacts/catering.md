# Catering Plan

## Dietary Requirements Recap

- 80 guests, Saturday 2026-12-12, 18:00-23:00 (5 hours), fully indoor, elegant but relaxed winter party at Balcony am Fernsehturm (recommended venue, unchanged after the requirements and venue revision).
- Food format: seated or buffet dinner plus snacks. Drinks (wine, beer, soft drinks) are in the budget.
- Dietary constraints: vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests. No counts were given, so all three groups are assumed present and every one must be fully covered. No specific counts are invented. The menu is sized so that every course has a vegan and a gluten-free choice for all 80 guests.
- Venue rule: Balcony am Fernsehturm allows outside food and alcohol, and its in-house catering is optional (not mandated). Any outside catering or corkage fee is unverified (estimate). Venue page: https://venuu.com/de/de/venues/balcony-am-fernsehturm
- Revision note: the requirements now only need a step-free entrance and accessible toilets (stage access relaxed). The accessible toilets are an open item with the venue, to be confirmed in the booking quote. This does not change food or drink. The buffet is on one level near the lifts, and a staff member can serve guests who cannot queue (service staff already costed).
- Budget: the whole event is capped at 12000 EUR including 10% contingency (10800 EUR planned). The target for catering is about 4800 EUR gross or less.
- Program times used: dinner service 19:00-19:50 (mains finished before 20:00), no food service during the awards (20:00 to about 20:20), late snack about 21:15-22:15.

## Menu Proposal

Based on the heycater buffet network, using the "Green Berlin Buffet" style (vegan-first, so one buffet serves everyone). Dish names are illustrative; the final menu is fixed with the quoted caterer.

Service timetable (aligned to the program constraints):

| Time | Service |
|---|---|
| 18:00-19:00 | Arrival, welcome drink and canapes |
| 19:00-19:50 | Dinner buffet (starters, mains, dessert table open); mains finished by 19:50 |
| 19:50-20:20 | Kitchen and service pause; no food service during the awards (20:00-about 20:20). Only drinks are available at the bar, with no clearing or serving noise at the tables. |
| 20:20-21:15 | Dessert table and coffee/tea remain open for self-service; live music |
| after 21:15 (about 21:15-22:15) | Late snack, self-service |
| to 23:00 | Bar only |

- **Welcome (18:00-19:00):** sparkling wine or elderflower sparkle (non-alcoholic), with vegan canapes (beetroot hummus cups, tomato-basil crostini on gluten-free bread).
- **Starters (buffet, 19:00-19:50):** seasonal winter salad with roasted root vegetables and pomegranate (vegan, gluten-free), pumpkin-ginger soup in cups (vegan, gluten-free), a cheese and antipasti board (vegetarian; gluten-free cracker option).
- **Mains (buffet, 19:00-19:50):** vegan lentil and vegetable stew or lentil patties with potatoes (vegan, gluten-free), a vegetarian gratin or baked polenta with mushrooms (vegetarian, gluten-free), plus one meat or fish main (for example chicken with roasted vegetables, allergens labelled). Sides: roasted potatoes, seasonal vegetables, rice or quinoa (vegan, gluten-free). The buffet is run as two parallel lines so 80 guests are served within 50 minutes.
- **Dessert (from 19:45, open until about 21:00):** fruit and vegan chocolate mousse (gluten-free), baked apple, a gluten-free brownie, cookies and stollen. Coffee and tea.
- **Late snack (about 21:15-22:15):** small finger food or mini rolls with spreads, with a vegan and a gluten-free choice (for example mini falafel, mini quiche, vegan sliders), self-service so no staff action is needed during any program item.
- **Drinks (18:00-23:00), package of about 14 EUR per head:** sparkling wine on arrival, one house red and one white wine, Pils, alcohol-free beer, still and sparkling water, juices, soft drinks, coffee and tea. A simplified package (no wheat beer, no second wine type) keeps the cost down. Final supply is billed by package or consumption; no drinks supplier is named yet.
- **Service notes:** every dish labelled for vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and main allergens. Gluten-free dishes prepared separately and served first with their own utensils. Order well ahead for a December Saturday peak date (the gluten-free page advises 5-7 business days at minimum). Buffet tables and the bar should be placed step-free and with wheelchair-passable aisles; to be agreed with the venue layout.

## Cost Summary

Basis: 80 guests. Food price is the heycater "Green Berlin Buffet" net price confirmed on the fetched page; other lines are estimates marked below. No cost change results from the venue and requirements revision.

| Item | Basis | Net | Gross (incl. 19% VAT, unverified (estimate) VAT rate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dinner buffet | 80 x 25.90 EUR net per head (sourced) | 2072.00 EUR | 2465.68 EUR |
| Dessert and late snack extras | 80 x 4.00 EUR net per head (unverified (estimate), below the 19.90 EUR full finger food menu price, as a small add-on) | 320.00 EUR | 380.80 EUR |
| Service staff | 3 staff x 5 hours x 39 EUR per hour (unverified (estimate), based on the 39 EUR per hour rate on the Bella&Bona pricing guide); 19:00-22:15 service plus setup | 585.00 EUR | 696.15 EUR |
| Drinks package (wine, beer, soft drinks, water, coffee) | 80 x 14 EUR per head (unverified (estimate); Bella&Bona guide gives 4-12 EUR per head for beverages in general, so 14 EUR allows wine and beer over 5 hours) | not split | 1120.00 EUR |
| Equipment (cutlery, glasses, platters) | unverified (estimate); may be booked separately according to heycater | included | included |

- **Total catering (food, staff, drinks):** about 4663 EUR gross (2465.68 + 380.80 + 696.15 + 1120.00 = 4662.63), about 58 EUR per head. This is below the 4800 EUR target, leaving about 137 EUR of headroom.
- **Included:** buffet food, dessert and late snack, service staff, drinks package. Delivery is generally included for professional caterers (Bella&Bona pricing guide). Glassware and equipment hire is unverified (estimate).
- **Not included:** coat check staff, music, decor, venue hire, venue corkage or fee for an outside caterer (unverified (estimate), possibly additional). Contingency is not included, as the budget agent adds it.
- **Risk range:** a 7% VAT rate on food would lower the food lines by about 300 EUR; the "International" buffet at 32.90 EUR net would raise the cost by about 670 EUR gross and would break the target, so it is not used.
- All items marked unverified (estimate) must be confirmed through the quote request.

## Recommended Pick

**heycater buffet (Green Berlin Buffet style, vegan-first, vegetarian and gluten-free variants) with service staff and a 14 EUR per head drinks package. Total about 4663 EUR gross (about 58 EUR per head), 80 guests.** It has a sourced per-head price (25.90 EUR net), vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free variants, and the venue allows outside catering. Fallback: Bella&Bona (own kitchen, delivery, setup and service included), whose price must be quoted (estimated 30 EUR net per head, which would break the target). The budget should use 4663 EUR.
