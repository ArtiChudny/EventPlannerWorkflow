# Venue Options

## Requirements Recap

- Corporate year-end party, 80 guests, Saturday 2026-12-12, 18:00-23:00 (5 hours), fully indoor, elegant but relaxed.
- Hard requirements (revised): capacity of at least 80 (seated or buffet dinner plus a stage or music area); step-free entrance; accessible toilets; central Berlin with good public transport. Step-free stage access is no longer required. A stage ramp or portable ramp is a nice-to-have.
- Accessible toilets must be confirmed from a real source or listed as an open item to confirm in the booking quote.
- Budget: 12000 EUR all-inclusive including 10% contingency (10800 EUR planned costs). The venue cost should stay at or below about 1500 EUR.
- Coat check needed. Extra setup and teardown time outside 18:00-23:00 must be costed.

## Candidate Venues

### Option 1: Balcony am Fernsehturm

- **Capacity:** 350 seated, 1,000 standing (flexible layouts 300-600), about 600 m2 main area (Venuu). Well above 80 guests. A zoned layout for 80 guests should be requested (unverified (estimate) whether offered).
- **Price:** Venuu lists day rental from 500 EUR (depends on event type). The zipcube listing shows "from 4,480 EUR" on an unclear basis, and eventlocations.com says price on request. The December Saturday price is therefore unverified (estimate). Planning figure 1500 EUR for the evening including setup (unverified (estimate), based on the Venuu "from" rate and peak-season demand). A written quote must confirm at most 1500 EUR.
- **Included in price (furniture, technician, projector):** Listed as available: projector/beamer, 7 m LED screen, sound system, lighting, PA, DJ equipment, stage with backstage and artist dressing rooms, dance floor. The space is furnished with lounge furniture and two bars, and more furniture is rentable (eventlocations.com). No page states whether furniture, technician and projector are in the base price or extra: unverified (estimate). Downstream planners should treat furniture, projector and screen as "available on site, inclusion to be confirmed" and the technician as not included until confirmed.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Step-free entrance and lift: confirmed. Four elevators ("barrierefrei ueber vier Aufzuege") on the Venuu, eventlocations.com and zipcube pages. Venuu also states "wheelchair accessible throughout". A further listing (bbfc-cloud.de, fetched) states "Step-free access: Yes" and "Lift: Yes".
  - Accessible toilets: unverified (estimate). No fetched page states them. The eventlocations.com page (German and English) lists only a plain "Toiletten" entry. The Venuu, zipcube and bbfc-cloud.de pages do not mention accessible toilets either. An earlier version of this file wrongly claimed the eventlocations.com page stated them. That claim is withdrawn. This hard requirement is open and must be confirmed in writing before booking.
  - Question to put in the booking quote request: "Is there at least one wheelchair-accessible toilet (step-free, adequate door width and turning space, grab rails) on the same floor as the event space or reachable by the lifts? Please confirm in writing and state its location."
  - Stage (nice-to-have only): stage height and ramp are not stated: unverified (estimate). Ask: "Can a portable ramp be provided for the stage, and at what cost?"
- **Parking:** 10 parking spaces (eventlocations.com listing). Central Alexanderplatz location (Alexanderstrasse 7). Public transport access assumed very good (unverified (estimate), not stated on the venue pages).
- **Indoor / outdoor:** Indoor main space (first floor) plus a 100 m2 terrace (not needed for this fully indoor event).
- **Rules and restrictions:** Own food and alcohol permitted (Venuu). In-house catering, beverages and staff available on request. A partner caterer (Koeche Kollektiv Berlin) and an on-site kitchen are mentioned by eventlocations.com. Outside catering or corkage fee: not stated: unverified (estimate). Cloakroom services and a reception area with main cloakroom are listed. Curfew and noise limits are not stated: unverified (estimate), must be confirmed.
- **Source:** https://venuu.com/de/de/venues/balcony-am-fernsehturm (additional details: https://www.eventlocations.com/de/venues/balcony-am-fehrnsehturm-berlin and https://www.bbfc-cloud.de/en/public/locations/balkony-am-fernsehturm)

### Option 2: Mokka Mitte Bar

- **Capacity:** Max. 100 seated, 150 standing, 150 m2 indoor space (plus a 300 m2 garden, not usable in December). Meets 80 guests.
- **Price:** Starting at 1,785 EUR per day including 19% VAT. This is already above the 1500 EUR target, and the December Saturday price is unverified (estimate). Screens, microphones, club sound and lighting are on site. Whether a technician or furniture is included is not stated: unverified (estimate).
- **Accessibility:** Listed as barrier-free and includes toilets. Step-free entrance detail and wheelchair-accessible toilets are not stated: unverified (estimate).
- **Parking:** Parking is available (listed); details and cost not given. Location at James-Simon-Park on the Spree, Berlin Mitte.
- **Indoor / outdoor:** Indoor event space, air-conditioned, with a separate outdoor garden.
- **Rules and restrictions:** 3 m x 1.2 m stage, club sound system, lighting, DJ equipment, microphones and screens on site. On-site kitchen with serviceable catering; dishes can be rented. Outside catering not stated: unverified (estimate). Smoking is allowed. The listing says there are no strict noise restrictions; curfew not stated.
- **Source:** https://www.eventlocations.com/de/venues/mokka-mitte-bar-berlin

### Option 3: Allabout Cafe Mitte

- **Capacity:** Maximum 80 seated in a 100 m2 space. This only just meets 80 guests and leaves no room for a stage or buffet area, so it is a weak fit.
- **Price:** Price on request: unverified (estimate); no figure is assumed.
- **Accessibility:** "Disability Access" is listed as an amenity, plus "Bathrooms". Step-free entrance and accessible toilets are not detailed: unverified (estimate).
- **Parking:** Parking spots available (listing); details not given. Located on Invalidenstrasse, Berlin-Mitte.
- **Indoor / outdoor:** Indoor.
- **Rules and restrictions:** In-house catering (focus on breakfast, brunch and lunch, vegetarian and vegan options possible); service staff and tableware bookable. Opening hours 9:00-22:00, extensions possible by agreement, so the 23:00 end needs confirmation. Equipment: sound system, projector, screen, microphone, podium, dance floor. No stage listed. Outside catering rules not stated: unverified (estimate).
- **Source:** https://www.eventlocations.com/en/venues/allabout-cafe-mitte-berlin

## Recommended Pick

**Balcony am Fernsehturm.** It has the strongest step-free evidence (four lifts on several pages, "Step-free access: Yes"), ample capacity, an existing stage with sound, lighting, projector and screen, the lowest listed base price, and it allows outside food and alcohol. With stage access no longer required, the remaining hard requirement is accessible toilets. This is **not confirmed by any source**. It is an open item that must be confirmed in writing in the booking quote (question above) before the booking is made. No other candidate does better on this point, so switching would not improve the evidence.

- **Estimated venue cost:** about 1500 EUR for the evening including setup and teardown (unverified (estimate); Venuu base is from 500 EUR per day, other listings suggest higher figures, so a quote is needed to stay at or below 1500 EUR).
- **Furniture / technician / projector:** projector, screen and lounge furniture are available on site; inclusion in the price is unconfirmed; assume a technician is an extra cost.
- **Catering rules for downstream planners:** outside caterer allowed; in-house catering, beverages and staff are optional. Any outside catering or corkage fee is not stated: unverified (estimate).
- **Curfew and noise:** not stated; assume the 23:00 end fits but confirm. Allow extra hire time for setup and teardown.
- **Fallback:** Mokka Mitte Bar if Balcony cannot confirm accessible toilets or a price near 1500 EUR (its base price is already 1,785 EUR, and its accessible toilets are equally unverified).
