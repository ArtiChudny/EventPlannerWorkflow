# Requirements

## Event Overview

- **Event type:** Corporate year-end party, with live music and a short award ceremony
- **Date:** 2026-12-12 (Saturday)
- **City / location:** Berlin, Germany. Central Berlin with good public transport access.
- **Guest count:** 80
- **Duration / hours:** 5 hours, evening event, 18:00-23:00
- **Style / theme:** Elegant but relaxed winter party, no strict dress code

## Budget

- **Maximum budget:** 12000 EUR (all-inclusive: venue, food, drinks, music, decor/AV, staff; includes contingency)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Contingency policy:** 10% unless the user says otherwise. The 12000 EUR limit includes the contingency, so planned costs must not exceed 10800 EUR before contingency.

## Venue Needs

- **Indoor / outdoor:** Fully indoor (December). Weather is only a brief note.
- **Accessibility needs:** Step-free entrance and accessible toilets only (relaxed by the user after validation). Step-free access to the stage is no longer a hard requirement. A ramp or portable stage ramp is a nice-to-have, to be confirmed with the venue. Accessible toilets remain required and must be confirmed from a real source or marked as an open item to confirm in the booking quote.
- **Fixed venue supplied by user:** none (find 2-3 options and recommend one; central Berlin, good public transport; capacity of at least 80 guests for seated or buffet dinner plus a stage or music area)

## Dietary Constraints

- Mix of vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests. The user gave no counts per constraint (see Open Assumptions).
- Food format: seated or buffet dinner plus snacks.
- Drinks (wine, beer, soft drinks) are included in the budget.

## Must-Haves

- Live music: live band or jazz/acoustic duo. A DJ only if the budget allows.
- Award ceremony of about 20 minutes with 5-8 awards.
- Basic decoration plus sound and lights for the music and the awards.
- Coat check included.
- Dinner (seated or buffet) plus snacks, and drinks (wine, beer, soft drinks).
- Staff included in the budget.
- Venue with a step-free entrance and accessible toilets (confirmed from a real source or listed as an open item to confirm in the booking quote).
- Central Berlin location with good public transport.
- Nice-to-have (not required): a ramp or portable stage ramp for the stage, to be confirmed with the venue.

## Hard Limits

- Maximum total budget 12000 EUR including contingency and all cost categories.
- Guest count 80 must fit the venue capacity.
- Event window 18:00-23:00 on 2026-12-12 (5 hours). The program must fit within it, without overlaps.
- Venue must have a step-free entrance and accessible toilets. Step-free stage access is not a hard limit.
- Fully indoor event.
- No category is explicitly excluded by the user. A DJ is conditional on the budget.

## Open Assumptions

- Start and end times 18:00-23:00 were confirmed by the user's acceptance of the defaults.
- Dietary counts per constraint (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free) were not given. Planners should assume that all three groups are present and must be fully covered, and should not invent specific counts.
- No allergies beyond vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free were stated. Alcohol-free options are covered by the soft drinks.
- Whether guests are standing or seated is not fixed. Seated or buffet dinner is acceptable.
- Venue setup and teardown time outside 18:00-23:00 is not specified. Any extra hire time is to be costed within the budget.
- Dress code: none strict.
- No travel or accommodation for guests is included. Only the event costs are within the budget.
- Language of the program is not stated and is assumed to be English and/or German as suits the company.
