## Speeches and Ceremony

- **Welcome speech (18:45-19:00):** a company executive or managing director, 15 minutes, short remarks with thanks for the year and an overview of the evening.
- **Award ceremony (20:00-20:20):** 20 minutes, 5-8 awards, about 2.5 minutes per award. The host acts as MC, with a presenter (executive or team lead) handing over each award on stage. Format: category announced, a one-line citation, short acceptance, photo on stage. Step-free stage access is no longer required; if the venue can provide a portable ramp it is a nice-to-have, otherwise an award recipient who cannot use the stage steps can be presented at floor level in front of the stage with the same microphone and photo.
- **Closing words (22:45-23:00):** the host, 15 minutes in total (about 5 minutes of thanks and practical information such as coat check and transit home, then farewell and coat collection).
- Language is English and/or German as the company prefers (not fixed in the requirements).
