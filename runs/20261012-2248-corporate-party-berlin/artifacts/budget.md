# Budget

## Limit and Assumptions

- **Limit:** 12000 EUR, including contingency (requirements.md). Currency: EUR. Contingency rate: 10% (requirements.md states no other rate). Planned costs must therefore not exceed 10800 EUR before contingency.
- **Revision note:** recomputed from the current versions of all artifacts after the logistics source fixes. The logistics total (662 EUR) is unchanged, and the venue, catering, program and decor-av cost summaries were re-read with every figure unchanged. The result is the same as before.
- **Artifacts used:** requirements, venue, catering, program, decor-av, logistics (all current versions). None is missing. All figures are in EUR, so no conversion was needed.
- **Source of each figure:** the "Recommended Pick" and "Cost Summary" sections of each artifact. No figure was changed or invented.
- **Catering:** gross figures (incl. 19% VAT, itself unverified (estimate)) from the catering Cost Summary, rounded to whole euros (2465.68 to 2466, 380.80 to 381, 696.15 to 696, drinks 1120). Category total 4663 EUR, matching the catering pick of about 4663 EUR (exact sum 4662.63).
- **Decor and AV:** the total of 1850 EUR from decor-av.md. That artifact assumes the venue supplies in-house AV, tables, chairs and lounge furniture. If the venue does not, this total rises. If the venue also includes a technician and microphones, it falls to about 1300 EUR. The 100 EUR reserve covers the nice-to-have stage ramp.
- **Logistics:** 662 EUR (coat check 308, accessibility assistance 154, GEMA 100, taxi vouchers 100). Security, permits, insurance and parking are 0 EUR in logistics.md.
- **Not costed anywhere (risk, not in totals):** venue corkage or outside-caterer fee, a Gestattung permit (up to 869 EUR), a one-day liability policy (about 150 EUR, only if the company policy does not cover the event), a temporary accessible toilet unit if the venue cannot confirm one (no source, no quote), extra caterer staff hours (39 EUR per hour), and venue-supplied furniture if not included. These would be covered only by the contingency.
- Almost every figure is an unverified (estimate); see the Basis column.

## Overrun Notes

No overrun. The margin is thin and rests almost entirely on unverified (estimate) figures, so real quotes could change the result. The largest shares of the 10445 EUR subtotal are catering (4663 EUR, 44.6%), decor and AV (1850 EUR, 17.7%), entertainment (1770 EUR, 16.9%), venue (1500 EUR, 14.4%) and logistics (662 EUR, 6.3%). The main risks that would push the total up are the unconfirmed venue price (a quote above 1500 EUR), venue-supplied furniture and technician not being included (decor-av-planner), the band fee (program-planner), and the uncosted items listed in Limit and Assumptions, which the 1044.50 EUR contingency would have to absorb.
