# Garden Wedding - Barcelona, Saturday 3 July 2027

## Overview

A garden wedding for 60 guests on Saturday 3 July 2027, with the ceremony at 17:30, dinner from about 20:30 and dancing outdoors until midnight. The recommended venue is Hostal Rural Mas Blanc in Sant Martí de Centelles, outside Barcelona city, which has 220 seated and 220 standing capacity and an outdoor garden. Its listing shows wheelchair access and accessible toilets, but the step-free route, accessible seating and toilet location still need written confirmation and an on-site check. The planned budget is a grand total of 22,119.90 EUR against the 22,200 EUR limit (including the 10% contingency), leaving 80.10 EUR of headroom. Many lines are unverified (estimate), so the total is a planning figure, not a quote.
