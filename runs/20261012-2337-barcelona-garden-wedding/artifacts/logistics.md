**Hourly rate basis (sourced for hospitality-type roles).** The Barcelona hospitality collective agreement (interprovincial convenio for hostelería y turismo de Catalunya, Barcelona tables in Annex II.A) sets the Nivel III category (camarero or camarera) at a monthly base pay of 1,607.25 EUR (group D) to 1,803.05 EUR (group A). This is taken from a fetched secondary summary (Turnozo, 2026 Barcelona convenio page, fetched 2026-10-13: https://turnozo.com/es/blog/convenio-hosteleria-barcelona-2026.md). The summary states 14 annual payments (two extra payments, in July and December) and 1,783 annual working hours, and a 25% increase for work between 22:00 and 06:00. Converted to a gross base hourly rate using the summary's own formula (monthly base x 14 / 1,783):

- Group A (top of range, used as the planning rate): 14.16 EUR per hour; night hours (22:00 to 06:00) at 17.70 EUR per hour.
- Group D (bottom of range, reference only): 12.62 EUR per hour.

Limits of this rate, all unverified (estimate): the primary DOGC text was not fetched (the summary cites DOGC núm. 9630 of 23/03/2026, which is not checked against the official gazette); the group that applies to Hostal Rural Mas Blanc and to these staff is not verified; the rate is gross base pay only, so employer social security and any meal or transport complements are not included; and whether a hospitality agreement applies to greeters, set-up and transport roles (rather than another agreement or an agency rate) is not verified. The rate is used as the sourced reference for all hospitality-type roles below. A written agency quote is required before the budget is final.

Day-of coordinator: one person, 14:00 to 00:30 (10.5 hours), at 25 EUR per hour, about 263 EUR (unverified (estimate); this is a supervisory role outside the hospitality category, so no convenio rate is applied). This person owns the run-of-show, the weather checks at 15:00, 18:30 and 21:30, and the go or no-go calls with the venue.

Greeters and hosts: two people, 17:00 to 00:30 (7.5 hours each): 5 day hours at 14.16 EUR and 2.5 night hours at 17.70 EUR, about 115 EUR each, about 230 EUR for both. They greet guests at the drop-off and parking, direct guests to the ceremony and to shade, and guide guests to the tent if the backup is activated.

Accessibility assistant for the two guests with walking aids: one person, 16:30 to 00:30 (8 hours): 5.5 day hours and 2.5 night hours, about 122 EUR. This person walks the step-free route with the two guests, keeps it clear, and assists at the accessible seats and toilets. A family member could fill this role instead, and the couple decides. It depends on the venue's written route confirmation (venue.md conditions 1 to 4).

Transport marshal: one person, 21:00 to 00:30 (3.5 hours): 1 day hour and 2.5 night hours, about 58 EUR. This person manages the pre-booked return vehicles and the requests for the accessible vehicle.

Audio technician for the ceremony microphone and speakers: one person, 16:00 to 18:30 (2.5 hours), at 25 EUR per hour, about 63 EUR (unverified (estimate); not a hospitality role). Remove this line if the venue sound system or the band's own kit covers the ceremony (decor-av.md).

Set-up crew: two people, 09:00 to 15:00 (6 hours each), day rate 14.16 EUR, about 170 EUR for both. They place decor and the aisle, check the accessible seat positions, and mark the dance area. The tent is erected by the covered backup supplier, not by this crew.
