## Outdoor Elements and Weather Backup

The weather outlook (weather.md) rates outdoor risk as Medium. Heat is the main factor: about 1 in 4 days in the five-year record reached 30 C or more, and the 17:30 ceremony falls in the hottest part of the day. Rain is the moderate factor: about 3 in 10 days had 1 mm or more, usually brief. The rain and heat thresholds and the decision times below are planning proposals from the weather outlook and are not sourced data (unverified (estimate)).

Covered backup: the venue listing states that tents can be rented "para proteger de cualquier cambio climático" (tent rental for protection against any weather change), but gives no price, size or conditions, and lists no indoor room (https://venuu.com/es/en/venues/hostal-rural-mas-blanc-zona-exterior, fetched 2026-10-13). The covered backup is therefore a separately sourced structure, see venue.md, which holds its size, capacity, price basis and date availability. Its price is not a quote for this event and is not repeated here. Whether its price includes VAT, transport, setup and removal, furniture, dance floor, band space, lighting or flooring is not confirmed, and its availability on 3 July 2027 is unconfirmed (see venue.md).

Fit risk: 60 guests seated for dinner use most of the covered backup's floor area, which may leave little or no room for a dance floor and the band's equipment (size and capacity figures: see venue.md). The covered backup may need to be larger or a second structure added. This is unverified (estimate) until the supplier confirms the size in writing and the venue confirms the site, ground fixings, power and access route.

Schedule impact of the covered backup: the backup stands in the same garden, so a switch to it keeps every segment time unchanged (17:30 ceremony, 20:30 dinner, 21:50 music start, and midnight end). Only the seating and the dance floor move under cover. The backup is planned to be erected before the 17:30 ceremony, with erection finished by 15:00 on Saturday 3 July 2027 (unverified (estimate); the set-up time is not from a source and must be confirmed with the supplier and the venue). The supplier's weekend install terms (see venue.md) would put erection on Friday 2 July rather than Saturday, so the erection booking is decided at the Friday 2 July 12:00 check. The shade from the covered backup also serves as the main heat measure for the ceremony and dinner.

On-site step-free route check (required before the event, outside the timed run-of-show): the step-free route from arrival to the ceremony area, the dinner and dancing area, the accessible toilets and the covered backup is not confirmed by any fetched source. The venue listing shows "Wheelchair accessible" and "Accessible toilets" but says nothing on step-free access, lifts or ramps. The couple or the planner must walk the route on site with venue staff before the event, check each level change, confirm the two accessible seats for guests with walking aids at the ceremony and dinner, and confirm the route to the covered backup. If the check fails, the venue's written access arrangements are needed, or the couple must decide whether to widen the search.
