# Venue Options

## Requirements Recap

- **Event:** Outdoor garden wedding, 60 guests, Barcelona, Saturday 3 July 2027.
- **Timing:** Ceremony 17:30; dinner about 20:30; dancing outdoors until 00:00 (midnight).
- **Budget (revised 2026-10-13, cycle 3):** 22200 EUR including the 10% contingency (raised from 20000 EUR, then 21500 EUR, then 22200 EUR). The 22200 EUR limit covers venue, food and drink (drinks in scope), music, photography, decor, transport and contingency. The budget-aggregator owns the total.
- **Venue must have (revised 2026-10-13):** A garden setting for the ceremony and the dinner, seating or standing space for 60 guests, wheelchair accessibility and accessible toilets as listed on the venue page (hard requirements, so that the two guests with walking aids can attend), and a priced covered or indoor backup space within the total budget.
- **Relaxed on 2026-10-13 (no longer hard requirements):** a confirmed step-free route from arrival to the ceremony and dinner areas, and confirmed accessible seating. These are recorded as a stated risk, with an on-site check of the route before the event.
- **Drinks (in scope for this revision):** Wine and drink service is included in catering. The venue supplies food and alcohol (own alcohol not allowed), so drinks must be bought through the venue. Whether drinks are included in the per-person package is not stated on the page (see Option 1, Price).
- **Venue not fixed by user:** 2 to 3 garden venues compared, one recommended.
- **Catering and music:** A small band or DJ with basic decor, lighting and sound is needed. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are required.
- **Revision (user decision, 2026-10-13):** Hostal Rural Mas Blanc stays the recommended venue. Its venue page was re-fetched on 2026-10-13 and still lists "Wheelchair accessible" and "Accessible toilets". Hotel Edelweiss is kept as the second option and La Barcelonesa is kept only as a comparison row.
- **Research scope:** Venues were searched on WebSearch and each option's own listing page was fetched. Where a figure was not on the page it is marked `unverified (estimate)`.

**Accessibility status against the revised hard requirements:** Hostal Rural Mas Blanc lists "Wheelchair accessible" and "Accessible toilets" under "In the venue" (fetched 2026-10-13), so it meets the revised hard requirement as listed on its page. Two points remain a stated risk and are not confirmed by any fetched source: the step-free route from arrival to the garden ceremony and dinner areas, and accessible seating. The location of the accessible toilets is also not stated. These must be checked on site before the event.

**Backup status (priced-backup requirement, retry 2 on 2026-10-13):** The venue's own page lists tents that can be rented against bad weather, but gives no tent price, tent size or indoor room. The venue's own tent is therefore still unpriced, and no indoor room is listed. A separate Barcelona tent supplier, Premiumtents, was re-fetched on 2026-10-13 and the page loaded. The tent has not been confirmed for the Mas Blanc site, and the venue's own tent rules are not stated.

## Recommended Pick

**Recommended: Option 1, Hostal Rural Mas Blanc.** On the revised requirements it meets the capacity and wheelchair/accessible-toilet hard requirements as listed on its fetched page. The step-free route and accessible seating are a stated risk, not a hard requirement, and must be checked on site before the event.

Why Hostal Rural Mas Blanc:

- Verified capacity of 220 seated and 220 standing comfortably covers 60 guests.
- It lists "Wheelchair accessible" and "Accessible toilets" under "In the venue" (re-fetched 2026-10-13), which Hotel Edelweiss does not list for wheelchair access. These tags are not evidence of a step-free route.
- It has an outdoor garden and open-air space for the ceremony and dinner. It is the only option that offers tents for weather protection, and a tent supplier with a sourced starting price and a banquet capacity of 74 is available for the backup.
- The listed Friday and Saturday availability (00:00 to 02:00) covers the 17:30 to 00:00 event on Saturday 3 July 2027.
- It publishes a per-person package price (from 42 EUR), so the food and drink cost can be estimated, although package contents are unverified. Drinks are supplied by the venue, so the drinks line must come from the venue's package or price list.

Estimated venue cost: about 5320 EUR excluding VAT and extras (2800 EUR rent plus 60 x 42 EUR package, both "from" prices, unverified (estimate)). Any separate drinks charge is not included and is unverified.

Covered backup price for the budget (retry 2 on 2026-10-13, unchanged by the 22200 EUR revision): **from 2000 EUR** (sourced: "Precio desde 2.000€", Premiumtents, https://www.bodas.net/carpas/premiumtents--e299577, fetched and confirmed 2026-10-13). This is a starting price for a 68 m2 tent that seats up to 74 at banquet. It is not a quote for this event and is not a figure for the full backup. It replaces the unsourced 1,000 EUR allowance and may be used as the tent line only if the budget-aggregator accepts a "from" price. Not in the sourced figure, and still unverified (estimate): VAT (the page shows 21% IVA on invoices, so whether 2.000€ is VAT-inclusive is unknown), dance floor, band space, lighting, furniture and flooring, delivery to the Mas Blanc site, and date availability (page shows "Reservado"). Because the tent may not hold a dance floor and band for 60 guests, the final backup size and price must come from a written quote. The budget-aggregator owns the total against the 22200 EUR limit.

Trade-offs: the venue is outside Barcelona city, so guest travel needs to be planned. The venue has no listed indoor room, so the covered backup depends on a tent quote for this site. Drinks are venue-supplied only, so drink cost depends on the package or a written drinks price.

Stated risk: the step-free route from arrival to the ceremony and dinner areas is not confirmed, and accessible seating is not confirmed. The plan must include an on-site check of the route and seating before the event. If the check fails, the venue's own written access arrangements are needed, or the user must decide whether to widen the search.

Items to request in writing from Hostal Rural Mas Blanc (confirmations, not hard requirements, except items 4 and 5):

1. **Step-free route (risk):** A step-free route from parking or the drop-off point to the ceremony area and to the dinner and dancing area in the garden, with the ground surface described and any level change covered by a ramp or level path.
2. **Lift or level access (risk):** Whether any building used for the event, rooms or toilets is reached by a lift, and whether any step is on the route.
3. **Accessible seating (risk):** Accessible seating for two guests with walking aids at both the ceremony and the dinner, placed on the step-free route.
4. **Accessible toilets (hard requirement):** The location of the accessible toilets, confirmation that they can be reached from the event areas, and how many there are.
5. **Covered or indoor backup (hard requirement for pricing):** Written confirmation that a tent (Premiumtents or another supplier) can be erected at the venue for 60 guests seated for dinner with room for dancing and the band, with its exact size, its step-free access, date availability for 3 July 2027, and a written all-in price including VAT, dance floor, flooring, lighting and furniture. The current listing shows no indoor room.
6. **Package contents and price:** What the 42 EUR per person package includes (food, wine, drinks and drink service, tableware), whether VAT is included, the price for 60 guests, and what the 2800 EUR rent covers (date, hours, space).
7. **Drinks:** Whether wine and other drinks are included in the package or charged separately, and their price for 60 guests, since drinks are in scope for the 22200 EUR limit.
8. **Dietary menus:** Vegetarian options and gluten-free options for the stated guests, within the in-house catering.
9. **Music and curfew:** Music end time (midnight), noise limits, and whether dancing outdoors until midnight is allowed.
10. **Date and hours:** Confirmation that Saturday 3 July 2027 is available from 17:30 to 00:00.
11. **Heat plan:** Shade in the garden for the 17:30 ceremony, and any cooling option (fans, misting, shaded seating).
12. **Equipment:** Whether lighting, sound and power for a band or DJ are included, and where the band or DJ can set up.
13. **Parking and drop-off:** Parking capacity and where guest transport can drop off.

Fallback: Hotel Edelweiss (Option 2) has a larger garden and no per-person price, but it does not list wheelchair access, so it does not meet the revised hard requirement as listed. It should be used only if Hostal Rural Mas Blanc cannot confirm item 4 or item 5.
