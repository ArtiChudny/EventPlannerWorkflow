- **Guests:** 60 guests, Saturday 3 July 2027, Hostal Rural Mas Blanc (08592 Sant Martí de Centelles, outside Barcelona city). Ceremony 17:30 to about 18:00; welcome drinks and cocktail reception 18:00 to 19:30 (matches the 18:00 to 19:30 welcome window in program.md); plated dinner from about 20:30; drinks service continues through dancing until 00:00 (midnight).
- **Budget (revised 2026-10-13):** 22200 EUR maximum including the 10% contingency, covering venue, food and drink (drinks in scope), music, photography, decor, transport and contingency. The budget-aggregator owns the total. This plan's catering total (see Cost Summary) is an input to that total and is not a budget decision.
- **Vegetarian:** "some guests", exact count not stated by the user. This plan covers a vegetarian meal for every guest who requests one, with the count to be confirmed at RSVP.
- **Gluten-free:** 2 guests. They need gluten-free welcome canapés, starter, main, bread, dessert and a gluten-free beer or drink option, prepared and served with cross-contamination controls.
- **Drinks (requirement: included in catering):** Wine and drink service is included in catering (user Q&A answer 5). Basic drinks run from the 18:00 welcome drink through dancing until 00:00. Whether drinks are inside the venue package price is not stated on the venue page and must be confirmed in writing. The drinks line therefore stays in the plan as an unverified (estimate). Details are in the Cost Summary.
- **Venue constraint:** The venue page (fetched 2026-10-13) states "Food from venue", "Alcohol from venue" and "Meeting catering", and "own food not allowed" and "own alcohol not allowed". It does not say whether outside caterers are allowed. Outside caterers are therefore treated as not permitted, and the venue's in-house catering is the only plan usable without written permission from the venue. Source: https://venuu.com/es/en/venues/hostal-rural-mas-blanc-zona-exterior
- **Venue status (revised venue.md, 2026-10-13):** Hostal Rural Mas Blanc is the recommended venue, conditional on written confirmation of the step-free route, accessible seating, accessible toilet location and covered backup. The venue's own tent is still unpriced. The covered backup, see venue.md, is a venue cost, not a catering cost, and is not included in this plan's totals. This plan is valid only while Hostal Rural Mas Blanc is the venue. Any change of venue requires a catering re-run.

This changes where service happens, not what is served or what it costs in this plan. The in-house caterer must confirm the kitchen-to-backup route, the service station position and the power for hot holding and cold storage. Their written confirmation is an open issue (see Recommended Pick).

The venue page does not confirm a step-free route (it lists only "Wheelchair accessible" and "Accessible toilets"), so the venue must confirm it in writing.

## Menu Proposal

Proposed for the Hostal Rural Mas Blanc in-house catering, package tier. The venue does not publish dish names, so the dishes below are a proposal for the in-house team to confirm and adapt to the chosen package.

Service timing (17:30 to 00:00 window): ceremony 17:30 to about 18:00; welcome drinks and cocktail reception 18:00 to 19:30 (matches program.md); plated dinner from about 20:30; dancing and drinks service until 00:00.

Step-free service: all welcome, buffet and drinks stations, and the route from the kitchen and bar to the garden or the covered backup, must be step-free or ramped, so that the two guests with walking aids can reach every service point. The venue must confirm this in writing.

Covered backup service (if the covered backup is used, see venue.md): the same menu, timing and drinks are served inside the covered backup space. The in-house caterer must confirm in writing: the kitchen-to-backup route (distance, surface, step-free access), the position of the service and bar stations inside the backup, power for hot holding and refrigeration, and that the backup layout leaves room for the 60 guests seated for dinner with the dance floor and band area (layout is set by the backup supplier and venue, see venue.md). The covered backup is priced in venue.md, not here.

Hot-weather note: the ceremony is at 17:30 in early July. Chilled welcome drinks and shaded service points are proposed, subject to the venue's heat plan (see weather.md).

- **Welcome drinks (18:00 to 19:30, served in the garden or the covered backup):** Cava toast; a gluten-free-safe canapé selection in vegetarian and meat/fish versions, served to seats or at a step-free station; a non-alcoholic welcome drink (sparkling water with fruit or a soft drink) for all guests.
- **Starter (plated, all guests):**
  - Vegetarian and gluten-free: heirloom tomato and burrata salad with basil oil (confirm no wheat-based garnish).
  - Meat or fish: chilled gazpacho with prawn skewer (gluten-free preparation).
  - Vegan (optional request, not required by the user): gazpacho without prawn.
- **Main course (plated, guest chooses at RSVP):**
  - Vegetarian: wild mushroom and saffron risotto made with gluten-free stock, roasted seasonal vegetables.
  - Gluten-free meat or fish: grilled chicken breast with roasted potatoes and seasonal vegetables, or grilled sea bass with vegetable ragout (no flour thickening).
- **Bread:** Warm bread basket for all guests; a separately prepared gluten-free bread roll for each of the two gluten-free guests, served from a labelled, separate basket.
- **Dessert (plated, all guests):** Crema catalana (gluten-free recipe, confirm no flour), or a fresh fruit plate with sorbet for vegetarian and gluten-free guests if the kitchen does not approve the crema.
- **Drinks (to be confirmed as included in the package; priced as an add-on in the Cost Summary if not):**
  - Wine: white, red and rosé with dinner.
  - Cava for the toast.
  - Beer: standard beer plus one gluten-free beer option for the two gluten-free guests (brand to be confirmed by the caterer).
  - Soft drinks, still and sparkling water, juices.
  - Coffee and herbal tea after dessert.
  - Drinks service continues during dancing until 00:00.
- **Late-night snack and digestifs:** Not included in the cost below. Optional add-on, cost unverified (estimate).

Cross-contamination controls for the two gluten-free guests: separate prep surface and utensils, no shared fryer or grill plate, gluten-free bread and dessert kept in sealed containers, and a menu card that marks gluten-free dishes at each seat.

- **Dependency on the venue:** The pick stands only while Hostal Rural Mas Blanc is the venue. If the venue changes, this catering plan must be re-run.
- **Budget effect of the drinks check:** The search did not find a sourced drinks price, so the catering total is unchanged. The lower total (2772 EUR including VAT) depends on the venue confirming in writing that drinks are in the package; the higher drinks allowance can be reduced only by a written quote or by a change to the drinks scope, which is the user's decision.
