# Decor and AV Plan

## Decor Concept

- **Scope:** Basic decor only, as the user confirmed (requirements.md, Must-Haves: "Basic decor included"). No stationery, extra florals, themed installations or styling beyond the basic set are planned. Budget context: 22200 EUR total including 10% contingency (requirements.md, revised 2026-10-13).
- **Setting:** The venue is Hostal Rural Mas Blanc, the recommended venue in venue.md (conditional on the written confirmations listed there). Source: https://venuu.com/es/en/venues/hostal-rural-mas-blanc-zona-exterior (fetched 2026-10-13). The venue has an open-air space and a pool area on natural grass. The decor works with the existing greenery rather than covering it, using natural materials (wood, linen, greenery) and a simple neutral palette, because the user gave no colour or style preference.
- **Ceremony area (17:30 to 18:00):** Rows of chairs with a clear centre aisle, a simple greenery arrangement at the aisle end, and one ceremony focal point. Keep decor low so the view of the garden stays open. The aisle and the seats for the two guests with walking aids must sit on the step-free route, which the venue has not yet confirmed in writing (venue.md, items 1 to 3). Keep the aisle clear of loose decor so a wheelchair can pass; the required width is to be confirmed with the venue (unverified (estimate)).
- **Dinner area (20:30 to 21:40):** Basic table centrepieces (low greenery or flowers in small vases) on each table, so guests can see across the table. Keep the dinner tables clear of the pool edge and of the step-free route.
- **Dance area (live music from 21:50 to 00:00, per program.md):** No decor beyond the lighting plan in the next section. The venue page does not list a dance floor, so a dance floor is a conditional rental line (see Rentals).
- **Venue rules:** The venue page does not mention candles, open flame, confetti, petals or decoration rules (unverified). Do not plan candles, open flame, confetti or petals until the venue confirms in writing. Use LED or battery-powered table lights unless open flame is confirmed as allowed.
- **Covered backup, see venue.md:** If the ceremony or dinner moves into the covered backup, the same low centrepieces and lighting apply inside the covered space. A covered space sized for 60 seated guests may not leave room for a dance floor and band, so the decor plan assumes the dance floor is laid either inside a larger covered space or on a separate area; this must be confirmed with the supplier in writing. Date availability for 3 July 2027 is unconfirmed.

Program.md does not price ceremony music. The venue sound system, if confirmed as included, or the band's kit can cover this. - **Dinner (20:30 to 21:40):** Background music at low volume is not scheduled in program.md, which places the live sets from 21:50. Dinner music and any microphone for the optional toast must be confirmed with the band or the venue. The optional welcome toast (21:40 to 21:50) needs a microphone only if the garden is not quiet enough.
- **Dancing (live music 21:50 to 23:50, closing song to 00:00):** Band sound and dance-floor lighting. Music end time is 00:00 (requirements.md and program.md). The venue page does not state a curfew, music end time or noise limit (unverified).

The venue's "Professional lighting system" may cover part of this; its scope and whether it is included are not stated (unverified). No uplight price for Barcelona was found, so the uplight line is an estimate.
- **Covered backup lighting (only if the covered backup is used, see venue.md):** Tent lighting is an additional item that is not included in the backup's starting price and is not priced here (quote required; see venue.md).

**Recommended AV setup (to be confirmed by written confirmation or quote):**

1. The band's own sound system for the 21:50 to 23:50 sets, with the closing song run from the same system. If the venue sound system is confirmed as included and suitable, the band may use it instead, and the choice must be written down.

3. Garden uplights and dance-floor light, from the venue's lighting system or the band's offer if confirmed, otherwise hired separately (quote required). If the covered backup is used, its lighting is added to this item after a quote.
