# Original Request

Plan an outdoor garden wedding for 60 guests in Barcelona, Spain, on a Saturday in July 2027 (ceremony in the late afternoon, dinner and dancing outdoors until midnight). Budget 20,000 EUR. We want the ceremony and the dinner in a garden setting, a small band or DJ, and a photographer. Some guests are vegetarian and two are gluten-free. The garden must be reachable for two guests who use walking aids. Weather is our main worry: tell us how likely heat or rain is and what the backup is.

# Revision decisions (2026-10-13)

- Budget: the limit is raised from 20,000 EUR to 21,500 EUR (including the 10% contingency). Chosen by the coordinator at the user's request to "raise the budget"; the user may change the figure.
- Accessibility: the requirement is relaxed. The venue must still be wheelchair accessible and have accessible toilets as listed on its page, but a confirmed step-free route and confirmed accessible seating are no longer hard requirements. The unconfirmed route is recorded as a stated risk, with a plan to check it on site before the event.
- Venue: Hostal Rural Mas Blanc stays the recommended venue.
